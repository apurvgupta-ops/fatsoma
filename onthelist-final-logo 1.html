Usage Rules
Full logo vs icon
Use the full logo (clipboard + name) anywhere it will be seen at 80px or larger — website, app header, marketing. Use the icon-only version (clipboard + tick, no name) for the app icon, favicon, and anything below 80px.
The name is one unit
"On The List" sits as a single italic line. Never break it across two lines. Never make any word larger or a different colour. It is one name, treated equally throughout.
The clip is non-negotiable
The spring clip and arch above it must always be present until the logo is below 44px. It is what makes this a clipboard and not a rectangle. Never remove it at usable sizes.
Colour rules
Primary is always dark background, gold strokes, cream name. On white use gold-dim (#7a6340) for the mark and black for the name. On gold use pure black for everything. Never use any other colours.
